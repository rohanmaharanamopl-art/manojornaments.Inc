Our Heritage

55 Years of Achievements

  1. 1971

    Founding of Manoj Ornaments Inc

    Established as a family master goldsmith atelier in India, laying the foundation for 55 years of hallmarked purity and artisanal integrity.

  2. 1995

    Nationwide Wholesale Expansion

    Expanded production capacity to become a primary supplier to over 200 major retail jewelry showrooms.

  3. 2008

    25,000 Sq Ft Advanced Facility

    Integrated modern CAD/CAM technology, 3D wax printing, and laser welding while preserving hand-finished quality.

  4. 2018

    USA Export Division Launch

    Created dedicated product lines for North American jewelry retailers, complete with insured doorstep logistics.

  5. 2026

    Celebrating 55 Years of Excellence

    Four generations of master craftsmanship serving certified fine jewelry to customers and partners in India, the USA, UAE, Hong Kong and Brunei.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Manoj Ornaments Inc?

Manoj Ornaments Inc is a family goldsmith business founded in 1971 in India. We make handcrafted 14K and 18K gold jewelry with diamonds and gemstones.

Do you sell to both jewelry stores and individual customers?

Yes. We supply wholesale to jewelry retailers (B2B) and we also serve individual customers (retail, B2C). Contact us and tell us what you are looking for.

Which countries does Manoj Ornaments Inc supply?

We supply customers in India, the USA, the UAE, Hong Kong and Brunei, with insured doorstep logistics.

What gold purity do you offer?

We craft hallmarked 14K and 18K gold jewelry set with certified diamonds and gemstones.
