Our Heritage

55 Years of Achievements

  1. 1971

    Founding of Manoj Ornaments

    Established as a family master goldsmith atelier in India, laying the foundation for 55 years of hallmarked purity and artisanal integrity.

  2. 1995

    Nationwide Wholesale Expansion

    Expanded production capacity to become a primary supplier to over 200 major retail jewelry showrooms.

  3. 2008

    25,000 Sq Ft Advanced Facility

    Integrated modern CAD/CAM technology, 3D wax printing, and laser welding while preserving hand-finished quality.

  4. 2018

    USA Export Division Launch

    Created dedicated product lines for North American jewelry retailers, complete with insured doorstep logistics.

  5. 2026

    Celebrating 55 Years of Excellence

    Four generations of master craftsmanship serving certified fine jewelry to customers and partners worldwide.
